# Notices & Acknowledgements

clible would not exist without the generous work of people who have digitized, formatted, and freely shared the Bible in dozens of languages. This file acknowledges those contributions.

## seven1m/open-bibles

A huge thank you to [Tim Morgan (seven1m)](https://github.com/seven1m) for maintaining the [open-bibles](https://github.com/seven1m/open-bibles) repository — a curated collection of public domain and freely licensed Bible translations in standard XML formats. This project is the backbone of clible's English and Finnish translation support, and the quality and consistency of that work made building clible's parsers significantly easier.

## Beblia/Holy-Bible-XML-Format

Thank you to [Beblia](https://beblia.com) (Andrey, "Proud Slave of Christ") for the [Holy-Bible-XML-Format](https://github.com/Beblia/Holy-Bible-XML-Format) repository — an extraordinary collection of 1000+ Bible versions across 200+ languages, freely shared with no strings attached. The generosity behind that work is remarkable and enables clible to support a wide range of Spanish and other language translations.
